# Tank ability audit · 0.6.1

Each row covers one class-spellbook listing, grouped across ranks. The data includes records with no trainer level, which do not establish that an ability is obtainable. Added the tank profile’s additional spells to the same inventory. “Manual” means no tank recommendation currently exists. Passive and seal effects are not separate button presses. Purify and Cleanse use the visual dispel cue. Faerie Fire refers to the bear-usable version. Racial options depend on the character.
